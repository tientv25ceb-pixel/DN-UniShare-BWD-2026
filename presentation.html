01

The Problem

Every year at Da Nang University Village, tons of textbooks, dorm supplies, and study materials are abandoned when students graduate or move out. Meanwhile, thousands of new students buy the exact same items.

🏋
500+
tons of student items wasted yearly
📋
10K+
new students buying duplicates annually
02

Our Mission

Connect students who no longer need items with those who do. One shared item at a time.

🌱

Reduce Waste

Extend the lifecycle of student items and cut down landfill waste in the university community.

💰

Save Money

Help students access free or low-cost essentials and lighten their financial burden.

🤝

Build Community

Foster a culture of generosity and connection across Da Nang's university village.

03

How It Works

Three simple steps to start sharing.

01

Post an Item

Snap a photo, pick a category, describe the condition, and set a meetup point.

02

Discover & Request

Browse listings, filter by category, and send a request to claim an item.

03

Connect & Pickup

Get approved, meet at a safe campus location, and pass it forward.

04

Core Features

📝

Post Items

Optimized form with categories, condition, and campus meetup points.

🔍

Explore & Search

Multi-dimensional filters and real-time search to find what you need.

📄

Item Details

Large photos, condition info, location, and giver profile at a glance.

📩

Request System

Send request → Giver approves → Arrange pickup. Simple flow.

📈

Impact Dashboard

Real-time stats on items exchanged, waste reduced, and money saved.

Mobile First
